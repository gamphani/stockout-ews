# Data card: synthetic facility stock data

**This data is entirely synthetic. It describes no real facility, district stock position or person.**

## Purpose
Test an explainable stockout early-warning method before real data is available.

## What is simulated
- 200 facilities, 28 Malawi district names, invented facility IDs (FAC-001...).
- 4 medicines, 36 months (Jan 2023 to Dec 2025), about 28,800 rows.
- Facility size (small/medium/large) sets demand. Remote facilities have longer
  and more often delayed deliveries, and miss more monthly reports.
- Seasonality: malaria, diarrhoea and respiratory medicines peak in different
  months; the contraceptive is flat (a control).
- District-level supply shortages hit all facilities in a district at once.
- Stock follows an order-up-to rule (reorder to about 2.5 months of recent use).

## What this means for results
- Patterns found by the model are patterns written into the generator.
- The stockout rate (about 10%) was tuned to look plausible; it is a modelling
  choice, not a measured national rate.
- The remote-vs-urban gap exists by construction. An equity finding here shows the
  method works, not that real facilities behave this way.
- Month-end stock, consumption and lead times are what a facility would report.
  A missing report hides all of that facility-month (about 4.5% of rows).
